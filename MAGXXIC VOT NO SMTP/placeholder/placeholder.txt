================================================================================
   9. ENCODING & TRANSFORM
================================================================================

   [[BASE64_ENCODE(text)]]          Encode any text to Base64
                                    → [[BASE64_ENCODE(welcome)]] → d2VsY29tZQ==

   [[BASE64_DECODE(text)]]          Decode Base64 to text
                                    → [[BASE64_DECODE(d2VsY29tZQ==)]] → welcome

   [[UPPERCASE(text)]]              Convert to uppercase
                                    → [[UPPERCASE(hello world)]] → HELLO WORLD

   [[LOWERCASE(text)]]              Convert to lowercase
                                    → [[LOWERCASE(HELLO)]] → hello

   [[CAPITALIZE(text)]]             Capitalize each word
                                    → [[CAPITALIZE(hello world)]] → Hello World
